Account Deletion Request

How to Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your OnionGuard account and all associated data, please send an email to:

adikanathaniel@gmail.com

Include the following in your email:

  • Subject: OnionGuard Account Deletion Request
  • The email address associated with your OnionGuard account
  • Your registered name

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Diagnosis scan history and results
  • Analytics data linked to your account
  • All other personal data stored in our systems
Please note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be removed within 7 days of your request.

You Can Also Delete From the App

Go to Settings in the OnionGuard app and tap "Delete Account" to submit a deletion request directly.
