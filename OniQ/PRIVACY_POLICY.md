# OnionGuard Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 5, 2026**

## Introduction
OnionGuard ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered onion disease detection and freshness checking application built for smallholder onion farmers in Ghana. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Name, email address, phone number, and username provided during registration

### Camera and Photos
- The app accesses your device camera and photo gallery solely to capture or select images of onions for disease detection and freshness analysis. Images are processed locally on your device for disease detection and are not stored on our servers.

### Usage Data
- Scan results (disease type, confidence level) are stored to provide you with scan history and analytics

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide disease detection and freshness analysis
- To display your scan history and farm analytics
- To provide treatment recommendations in your preferred language
- To authenticate your account

## Data Storage
- Account data is stored securely in our database
- Scan results are associated with your email for history tracking
- We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties

## Third-Party Services
- Google Gemini API is used for freshness analysis (images are sent securely for analysis)
- No personal information is shared with third-party services beyond what is necessary for app functionality

## Data Security
- Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing
- Authentication uses JWT tokens
- All API communications use standard HTTP protocols

## Your Rights
- You can view your scan history within the app
- You can change your password at any time
- You can request account deletion by contacting us

## Children's Privacy
This app is not intended for use by children under 13.
